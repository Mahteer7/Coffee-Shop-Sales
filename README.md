# Coffee Shop Sales Analysis – Power BI Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing coffee shop sales data using **Microsoft Power BI** to identify sales trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, evaluate product performance, and compare store performance.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of key business metrics, helping management understand what drives revenue, when customers make purchases, which products perform best, and how different store locations are performing.

## Business Problem

A coffee shop business operating across multiple locations generates a large volume of sales transactions every day. However, without proper analysis, management may struggle to answer important business questions:

- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- During which hours does the coffee shop experience the highest customer demand?
- Which product categories contribute most to total sales?
- How do sales and customer footfall vary across store locations?
- Which days of the week generate the most orders?
- What is the distribution of orders based on customer purchasing size?

The objective of this project is to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights that support better operational and strategic decisions.

## Business Analysis & Key Insights

### 1. Sales Trends by Hour
- Analyzes product quantity ordered across different hours of the day.
- Identifies peak business hours and periods of lower customer demand.
- Helps management optimize staffing and inventory planning.

### 2. Product Category Performance
- Examines the percentage contribution of different product categories to total sales.
- Helps identify the categories driving the largest share of revenue.
- Supports product assortment and promotional decisions.

### 3. Order Size Distribution
- Analyzes the distribution of orders across Large, Regular, Small, and Not Defined categories.
- Helps understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Provides insights for pricing strategies and promotional campaigns.

### 4. Store Location Performance
- Compares total sales and customer footfall across different store locations.
- Helps identify high-performing and underperforming locations.
- Supports location-based operational improvements.

### 5. Top 5 Products by Sales
Identifies the five highest-revenue-generating products:

| Product | Sales |
|---|---:|
| Barista Espresso | $91,406.20 |
| Brewed Black Tea | $47,932.00 |
| Brewed Chai Tea | $77,081.95 |
| Gourmet Brewed Coffee | $70,034.60 |
| Hot Chocolate | $72,416.00 |

This analysis helps management understand which products contribute significantly to revenue and deserve greater attention.

### 6. Weekly Sales & Order Patterns
- Analyzes order activity across all seven days of the week.
- Helps identify variations in customer demand throughout the week.
- Supports weekly staffing, inventory, and promotional planning.

## Business Value

This dashboard helps coffee shop management:

1. **Optimize Operations:** Identify peak hours to improve staff scheduling and resource allocation.
2. **Improve Inventory Management:** Understand product demand to make better inventory decisions.
3. **Increase Revenue:** Identify top-performing products and categories for targeted promotions.
4. **Evaluate Store Performance:** Compare locations and identify opportunities for improvement.
5. **Understand Customers:** Analyze purchasing patterns to develop better marketing and pricing strategies.
